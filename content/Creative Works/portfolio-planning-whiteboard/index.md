I use whiteboards to plan things on a regular basis, and design one to plan my portfolio. It was originally designed in Microsoft Whiteboard for use during a Teams meetings, however Microsoft Whiteboards can only be shared to people/groups, not publicly. If you would like that version, please reach out to me at megan.barnes@unt.edu.

Otherwise, enjoy the <a href="https://www.canva.com/design/DAHAZ44-4uI/tNntiimIEkBh0U6qrDtYUg/view?utm_content=DAHAZ44-4uI&utm_campaign=designshare&utm_medium=link&utm_source=publishsharelink&mode=preview">Canva version</a> or the <a href="https://lucid.app/lucidspark/a7e82ee3-23be-4759-ab40-98f34884db37/edit?invitationId=inv_69dddc02-6a75-4fe3-bcb3-3c204db72167f">LucidSpark version</a>.  

I use Canva most often, but find that the whiteboard feature of Lucid is easier to work with now that I have built this template for multiple platforms. I particularly like the table of contents that allows you to quickly move from frame to frame making navigation much smoother.  

Enjoy!
